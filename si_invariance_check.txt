WHAT THIS SUPPORTS
  Influenza: 0 of 24 cells move. The onset does not depend on the serial
             interval, which is what the degenerate weekly kernel predicts,
             and it is why the five-mean SI sensitivity grid returns four
             distinct configurations rather than twenty.
  RSV:       3 of 12 cells move. The onset does depend on it.
  So the two halves of the pathogen contrast are not the same instrument.

WHAT IT DOES NOT SUPPORT
  The RSV effect rests on a small base: count the seasons, not the cells,
  before quoting it. The grids are unmatched (see the header). And nothing
  here bears on the within-pathogen signal-to-noise result, which does not
  use the kernel and is where the amplitude claim now sits.
